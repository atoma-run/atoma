## Adversarial design review

- Mutation survival disproves the particular order claim, not invoice correctness.
- Source order may meet a static requirement; never manufacture a behavioral test
  to distinguish observationally equivalent implementations.
- Explicit executed distinguishing tests still require execution and an assertion
  that would differ. A correct source snippet alone must not bypass this demand.
- Existing warehouse duplicate/audit, stateful prerequisites and keyboard focus
  incidents remain governed by their observed setup/action/assertion. No keyword
  classifier, mutation executor or automatic semantic refusal is introduced.
- Compound reviews must collect all visible gaps but cannot establish completeness
  for omitted source or invent requirements from validator remediation prose.
- A source guard or title is not proof of all schema boundaries; an aggregate is
  not proof of separately required components. The recorded currency-array bug
  remains evidence for behavioral checks, not a claim solved by this prompt.
- Shared analyst guidance must prevent approval from being cited as its own proof;
  it does not turn this limited replay into a measured analyst improvement.
