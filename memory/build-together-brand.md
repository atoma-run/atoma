# Build together — brand decision, 2026-10-22

The owner adopted **Build together.** as Atoma’s public signature and requested
it on the home page and in the Atoma Compute presentation.

Supporting Compute line: **Your ideas. Your computer. Atoma by your side.**

Explain the collaboration concretely: the customer brings goals, follows work
and guides what comes next; Compute lets their chosen computer execute work for
the projects they authorize, within their chosen resource budget. Do not imply
that customer computers form a shared pool or run other customers’ projects.

The unsigned beta notice and illustrated macOS installation guide remain explicit.
